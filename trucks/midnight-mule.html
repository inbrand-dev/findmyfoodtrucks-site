About Midnight Mule

Midnight Mule is a fictional mobile bar concept built to demonstrate how nontraditional mobile vendors can use Find My Food Trucks for live status, location updates, service details, and event availability.

The trailer provides bartending service for booked events. Hosts supply or select the alcohol package according to local requirements, while the crew handles setup, mixers, garnishes, ice, glassware options, and cleanup.
